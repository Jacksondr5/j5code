# Office Artwork Credits

This office uses artwork from [LPC Revised](https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC) by
Eliza Wyatt (DeathsDarling) and contributors, under the OpenGameArt Attribution
3.0 license (OGA-BY 3.0). License text: https://static.opengameart.org/OGA-BY-3.0.txt

The furniture PNGs are unmodified upstream images. The character sheets were
exported from the upstream LPC Revised layers at the upstream cell size and
frame order and are not byte-identical to the upstream files. The renderer
selects regions, composes character layers, and tints shirts, hair, and
trousers at runtime.
No endorsement by the artists is implied.

Sources:

- https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC/tree/main/Objects/Furniture
- https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC/tree/main/Objects/Small%20Items
- https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC/tree/main/Characters
- https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-revised-the-office

The sections below reproduce the upstream credit files verbatim: artists,
titles, sources, and per-item licenses.
