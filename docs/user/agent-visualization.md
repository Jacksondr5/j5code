# Agent visualization

The Visualization panel shows the agents working in a thread as people in a
small pixel-art office, so you can see at a glance who is busy, who is waiting,
and who has finished.

## Opening it

From a thread, open the right panel and choose **Visualization** from the plus
menu or the empty-panel launcher, or press **V** while the launcher is showing.
The panel is one tab per thread; opening it again focuses the existing tab.

## Reading the room

- Each agent has its own desk and sits at it while working. A small chip above
  the character shows its state: green working, amber waiting, red failed, grey
  idle.
- An agent whose task involves research sits at a reading table and shuffles
  its papers when it reports progress.
- Idle agents take a seat in the break area. Finished agents walk out. A failed
  agent is carried out by medics.
- When there are more agents than the room can seat, running agents are shown
  first and the picker reports how many are not shown.

The room is still whenever nothing is happening. It animates while an agent is
moving or a table is being used, and now and then for a small touch of life: an
idle agent stretching its legs, a puff of steam from the coffee machine, the
wall clock ticking over, an agent dozing off after a long break.

## Selecting an agent

Hover over an agent for its name, state, and current task. Click to keep the
details open and highlight its workstation, or pick an agent from the menu at
the top of the panel. Clear the selection with the close button on the details
card, or by clicking an empty part of the office.

## Closing it

Close the Visualization tab like any other right-panel tab. Reopening it later
brings the agents back in through the door.

Artwork credits are available from the **Art credits** link in the panel.
