# Inbox

The Inbox lists what your agents are waiting on you for: their questions, a Crew seat's approval
requests, and, for an Owner, worktrees an archived Crew left behind. Open it from the bell, which
counts what is waiting.

## Reading and answering a question

A question reads as it would in the agent's thread. On web and desktop, a link in it opens where it
would there: a pull request, a commit, a file or an artifact opens in that agent's own panel, shown
beside the Inbox. The panel is the thread's, so what you open there is still open when you go to the
thread. Clicking a link in another agent's question switches to that agent's panel. When the agent
has no thread you can open, its links open the way they do elsewhere in the app.

Type your answer under the question and send it as you send a message in the composer. Your answer
is delivered exactly as you typed it, with any images or files you attach. To talk it through
instead, open the agent's thread and reply there.
